An AI work exam at the end of your course

You teach. IAPQB runs the exam and issues the credential.

For training providers, coaching institutes, bootcamps and corporate trainers · Updated 16 November 2026

IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI is taken with AI switched on. It measures whether a person can use AI on a real task and check and fix what it produced. Partners register their learners as a group on terms set by agreement, and use our published standards and materials in class.

Write to usResearch reports

What partners get

  • An external exam for your course. Your certificate of completion sits next to a result from an independent body. Anyone can check our credential by its number.
  • Group terms. Set by agreement, by number of learners and period. Larger groups get better terms.
  • Teaching material. Our published standard, marking rubrics, practice test and research reports can be used in class without asking us. Reports are CC BY 4.0: credit the source and reuse them.
  • A tracked practice link. You get your own link to the free practice test, and we tell you each month how many people used it.
  • Group results. With your learners' consent, we send you their results by group, so you can improve the course.

How it works

  1. Write to us with your organisation's name, the course and the expected number of learners.
  2. We agree the terms: number of learners, period and price.
  3. You send us the list of learners (email addresses) and pay their fees under the agreement, and we give you an agreement code. The code only confirms the agreement: it is not a voucher and cannot be sold or passed on.
  4. Each learner registers with their listed email address and the code, and sits online at a time they choose within 90 days of registering (one free 90-day extension).
  5. Passing learners receive the credential, and you receive group results within the consent given.

Exams run on demand from 19 December 2026, in English or Korean, and learners can sit online from more than 150 countries. Dates, fees and rules are in examination notice No. 2026-1. Grades and registration: grades and registration.

When you advertise

  • Describe the credential as a private qualification registered in the Republic of Korea, with its registration number (No. 2026-008253). Do not present it as a national or government-accredited qualification.
  • Do not use "official IAPQB course", "IAPQB-certified course", guaranteed pass, guaranteed job or salary claims, or pass-rate claims.
  • Do not say that a course is required to sit the exam or makes it easier to pass. Anyone can sit; teaching and testing are kept apart.
  • If you pay people to promote your course, they must label it as an advertisement. In India this follows the ASCI influencer guidelines and the CCPA guidelines on coaching advertisements.
  • Handle learners' personal data only as far as registration needs.

Questions

Do we have to buy textbooks or courses from IAPQB?
No. IAPQB does not sell courses, textbooks or materials. Teach to the published standard in your own way.
Can our trainers sit the exam too?
Yes. Terms for trainers can be part of the agreement.
We are a university or a school.
See for universities and schools.
What does the exam assess if AI is on?
Using AI on a real task, and checking and fixing what it produced. All marking rubrics are published before anyone sits.
