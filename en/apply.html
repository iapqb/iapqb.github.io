Fees include Korean VAT and are charged in Korean won (KRW). US dollar figures are approximate; your card issuer converts at its own rate. The certificate fee is included, and there are no other charges. Scoring less than 40% of the points of any one subject is a fail, whatever the total. Foundation, Associate and Professional are scored automatically and can be taken on demand. Subjects and scoring rubrics are set out in IAPQB-STD-002 (Korean), and sample questions for each grade will be published on this page before registration opens.

Payment

Pay by card in your own name. We accept Korean cards and international cards (VISA, Mastercard, JCB, AMEX and others). Payments are processed by our payment service provider, Eximbay Co., Ltd. Your card statement may show EXIMBAY, the name of the payment service provider.

Exam deadline and refunds

  • Exam deadline: start your exam within 90 days of payment. Ask before the deadline and we extend it once by 90 days, free of charge. A registration not started by the deadline lapses and is not refunded. We remind you 7 days and 1 day before the deadline.
  • Refund rule: you may cancel at any time before you start, within the deadline, and we refund the full fee. The exam counts as started when the first question appears on your screen; after that there is no refund. You confirm this rule on screen before paying and again just before you start.
  • How to request a refund: press “Cancel registration” on your registration screen, or email contact@iapqb.org with your name, payment date and grade. We refund within 3 business days by cancelling the card approval.
  • If we cancel or postpone an exam, or a system failure interrupts it, you choose a full refund or a new sitting. If you could not sit because of hospitalization, quarantine, a death in your immediate family, a natural disaster or an internet shutdown in your area, send proof within 30 days of the exam date and we refund in full.
  • Strategist (scheduled sessions): full refund if you cancel within the registration period or up to 7 days before the exam date, 50% up to 3 days before, and no refund after that. Cancelling within 7 days of payment and before the exam is always refunded in full.

Steps

When registration opens, choose a grade on this page, enter your name, date of birth, phone number and email, and pay. Before paying you will see the exam deadline, the refund rule and the sample questions for your grade. After paying, book a slot for the ID check, show your photo ID on a live video call, and start the exam. We do not store ID images, face video or screen recordings.
