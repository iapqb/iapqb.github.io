Verify a certificate

IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI · Registered private qualification No. 2026-008253 (Republic of Korea)

Enter the certificate number and the issue date printed on the certificate to check that IAPQB issued it and whether it is valid now. The number alone does not return a result.

If it is found, you will see the name, grade, issue date and expiry date on the certificate. The name is exactly as the candidate entered it when registering. For a revoked certificate, only the revocation is shown, without the name.

Exam conditions

The exam is online and unproctored, and AI tools are allowed by design. Using AI during the exam is not misconduct; having another person take it for you is (Notice No. 2026-1).

Candidates are identified by the email address they register with, and the name on the certificate is exactly as the candidate entered it.

Certificates are issued to candidates who pass, once the official result is confirmed, and are valid for 3 years from the issue date (GOV-002 Articles 21 and 23). Full details are in Notice No. 2026-1.
