IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI · Foundation · Associate · Professional

Registration opens on 18 December 2026 at 10:00 for the notification list and on 19 December at 10:00 for everyone (Korea time, UTC+9). Exams run on demand from 19 December.

There is no ID check or video call, and you do not need a camera. We identify you by the email address you register with, and your certificate shows your name exactly as you enter it.
