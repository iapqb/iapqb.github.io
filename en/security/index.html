Report a security problem

If you find a weakness in our site or exam system, please tell us first and give us time to fix it. We will thank you by name if you wish.

IAPQB · Updated 24 November 2026 · Machine-readable contact: /.well-known/security.txt

How to report

  • Email contact@iapqb.org with "Security" in the subject.
  • Say what you found, the address or request involved, and the steps to reproduce it. Screenshots help.
  • If you saw someone else's data, tell us what kind it was. Do not keep it or pass it on.
  • We reply within 3 working days (Korea time) and tell you when the problem is fixed.

What is in scope

  • iapqb.org and all its pages.
  • The exam system: the registration, "My exam" and exam pages, and exam.iapqb.workers.dev.
  • Not in scope: the pages and systems of our payment provider, and of the other companies whose services we use (such as GitHub, Cloudflare and our email provider). Please report those to the company that runs them.

Please do not

  • Run denial-of-service or load tests, or send large volumes of automated requests.
  • Register, or make our system send email, to addresses that are not yours.
  • Look at, change or delete other people's data beyond the minimum needed to show the problem.
  • Try social engineering, phishing or physical attacks on us or on candidates.
  • Publish the details before the problem is fixed or before 90 days have passed since your report, whichever comes first.

Good-faith research

If you follow the rules on this page, we treat your research as authorised. We will not take legal action or make a complaint against you because of it, and if anyone else does, we will say that you acted under this page.

What is not a security problem

  • The exam is online and unproctored, and AI tools are allowed by design. Using AI during the exam is not misconduct; having another person take it for you is (Notice No. 2026-1). These are rules, not weaknesses. But do tell us if you find a way to see questions or answers you should not see, change a score, or use someone else's registration.
  • Results from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact, such as missing headers on static pages.

Rewards

IAPQB is run by one person and does not pay bug bounties. With your permission, we list your name or handle below.
