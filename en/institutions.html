Why schools are asking now

UNESCO published AI competency frameworks for students (12 competencies) and for teachers (15 competencies) in August 2024. In June 2026 the OECD and the European Commission published a framework for primary and secondary schools, Empowering Learners for the Age of AI, with 19 competences, and PISA 2029 will assess media and AI literacy for the first time. In the European Union, Article 4 of the AI Act has required providers and deployers of AI systems to ensure their staff have sufficient AI literacy since 2 February 2025.

Schools are being asked to teach AI literacy. Measuring it is harder. Our test measures one part that is hard to check at scale: using AI on a real task and checking what it produced.

Sources: UNESCO, students · UNESCO, teachers · OECD and European Commission framework · EU AI Act Article 4

How the free cohort benchmark works

  1. Email contact@iapqb.org with your institution's name, country and roughly how many students will take part.
  2. Within 3 working days we send you a short code and a link. Share it however you like: class group, LMS, placement drive.
  3. Students take the test on a phone or computer in about 12 minutes, in English, Hindi, Korean, French, Arabic, Portuguese or Spanish, with any AI they like.
  4. When 30 or more have finished, we email you the cohort report. Run it again next term to see the change.

Reports are aggregated only. Below 30 completions we send nothing, so no student can be picked out.

Students who want a credential

The test is practice and gives no certificate. Students who want a line for their CV can sit IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI, a four-grade qualification (Foundation, Associate, Professional, Strategist) taken with AI. Fees are published per candidate with reductions by country, and group terms for institutions are announced before each registration opens. The first round has not been announced yet; its dates and fees will be published at least 30 days before it runs. See questions and answers and grades and registration.

High schools

For high schools we offer the free anonymous test and class reports. Because the test collects no personal data, you can use it in class straight away. Paid registration for students under 18 needs a guardian-consent process, which we have not opened yet.

Teachers and staff

Adults can register for Foundation or Associate. A pass can serve as one record of staff AI literacy, for example when your institution looks at Article 4 of the EU AI Act. It is not a compliance certificate.

Training providers

You may teach to our published framework without asking us. We do not sell courses, textbooks or materials; we only run the exam, so teaching and testing stay apart.
