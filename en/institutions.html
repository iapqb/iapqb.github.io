Why schools are asking now

UNESCO published AI competency frameworks for students (12 competencies) and for teachers (15 competencies) in August 2024. In June 2026 the OECD and the European Commission published a framework for primary and secondary schools, Empowering Learners for the Age of AI, with 19 competences, and PISA 2029 will assess media and AI literacy for the first time. In the European Union, Article 4 of the AI Act has required providers and deployers of AI systems to ensure their staff have sufficient AI literacy since 2 February 2025.

Schools are being asked to teach AI literacy. Measuring it is harder. Our test measures one part that is hard to check at scale: using AI on a real task and checking what it produced.

Sources: UNESCO, students · UNESCO, teachers · OECD and European Commission framework · EU AI Act Article 4

What the exam measures

Four grades: Foundation, Associate, Professional and Strategist. Candidates may use any AI tool. Some questions can only be answered correctly by checking what the AI produced, so the exam rewards checking and fixing over recall. The top grade, Strategist, is read and judged by a person. Marking rubrics are published before anyone sits.

Working with your institution

  • Group registration. We agree terms with each institution: number of candidates, grades and schedule. Write to contact@iapqb.org.
  • Recruiters and placement. Anyone can check a credential by its number. Candidates will be able to add it to LinkedIn in one step from the first round.
  • Sample questions. Staff and students can see the question style in our practice test.

Research and open records

Our standards, rules and the data behind our research are public in our GitHub repository, together with the scripts that produce the figures, so anyone can check or reuse what we claim. Comments on the standards are kept in public there too.

Our research reports look at how courts, regulators, companies and researchers deal with AI at work, with every source listed and checked. Teachers may reuse them under CC BY 4.0. The series is archived on Zenodo with a DOI (10.5281/zenodo.24248199).

Fees and the first round

Fees are published per candidate, with reductions by country. The first round has not been announced yet; its dates and fees will be published at least 30 days before it runs. See questions and answers and grades and registration.

High schools

Registration for students under 18 needs a guardian-consent process, which we are preparing and have not opened yet. Teachers and other staff can register now.

Teachers and staff

Adults can register for Foundation or Associate. A pass can serve as one record of staff AI literacy, for example when your institution looks at Article 4 of the EU AI Act. It is not a compliance certificate.

Training providers

You may teach to our published framework without asking us. We do not sell courses, textbooks or materials; we only run the exam, so teaching and testing stay apart. Group registration codes for your learners: for training providers.
