What this qualification checks

Using AI at work is ordinary now. Employers still want to know whether a person checked what the AI produced and can stand behind the result. In the Associate and Professional exams, checking AI output carries the most marks (60 of 100 in the Associate practical, 35 of 100 in Professional).

How the exam works

  • Online, in Korea or abroad. All you need is a computer with an internet connection. Foundation, Associate and Professional are offered in Korean and English; you choose the language when you register, and the certificate is issued in that language.
  • No ID check, no camera. We identify you by the email address you register with. We do not check identity documents, hold video calls or record you or your screen.
  • AI tools. In practical exams you may use generative AI without restriction and submit a short log of the tools and key prompts you used; the log is not scored; it is used to investigate misconduct and, for registrations from 21 December 2026, also to improve questions and for statistics and research that identify no one. For written exams, the tools you may use are stated in the examination notice. Help from another person, and photographing, sharing or posting questions, is misconduct.
  • Results. Auto-scored results appear on screen as soon as you finish. Official results follow within 5 business days.

Certificates and verification

If you pass, you receive a digital certificate that can be checked for forgery or alteration (Open Badges compatible). We do not make plastic cards, to reduce plastic waste. Anyone can check a certificate at any time on our verification page using its number. A certificate is valid for 3 years and can be renewed.

Exam deadline and refunds

  • Start your exam within 90 days of payment. Ask before the deadline and we extend it once by 90 days, free. A registration not started by the deadline lapses without refund; we remind you 7 days and 1 day before.
  • Cancel any time before you start and we refund the full fee within 3 business days. The exam counts as started when the first question appears on your screen; after that there is no refund.
  • How to request a refund: press “Cancel registration” on your registration screen, or email contact@iapqb.org with your name, payment date and grade. We refund by cancelling the card approval.
  • If we cancel, postpone or have a system failure, you choose a full refund or a new sitting. Documented emergencies such as hospitalization or a death in the family are refunded in full.

Frequently asked questions

Is it a government-recognised qualification?
No. It is a private qualification registered under the Framework Act on Qualifications of the Republic of Korea (registration No. 2026-008253). It is not a national qualification or a government-accredited private qualification.
Can I take the exam now?
From 19 December 2026. Examination notice No. 2026-1 (Korean original) was published on 19 November 2026. Registration opens on 18 December 2026 at 10:00 Korea time (UTC+9; 06:30 in India) for people who signed up for the first-session notice, and on 19 December at 10:00 for everyone. Foundation, Associate and Professional run on demand in Korean and English; Strategist sessions will be announced separately.
Is there a free test I can try? Does it give a certificate?
Yes. The free AI work test has 10 questions in the style of the real exam, any AI allowed, and shows which areas need work. It gives no certificate: certificates go only to people who pass the real exam. Take the free test
Does it count for visas or immigration?
It is not on any country's visa points list. People use it on CVs and in interviews to show they can work with AI.
Do you sell courses or study material?
No. We keep teaching and testing apart, so we sell no textbooks or courses. The syllabus and marking rules are free to read.
Who can sit the exam?
Foundation and Associate are open to anyone. Professional needs an Associate pass or proof of equivalent work experience. Strategist needs Professional and has limited seats per round.
Do you record my face?
No. There is no ID check and no camera. We identify you by the email address you register with, and we do not receive ID images, face video or screen recordings.
Can I get a refund?
Cancel before you start and you get the full fee back. The full rules are under “Exam deadline and refunds” above.
Does the qualification expire?
A certificate is valid for 3 years from issue and can be renewed.
How do I put it on my CV?
Write the qualification name, grade, issuer and credential number, for example: “IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI Foundation · International AI Practice Qualification Board (Korea, registered private qualification No. 2026-008253) · Credential No. XXXX”.
What can I say about it in an interview?
For example: “I found a qualification aimed at people who work with AI, so I sat it. We could use AI during the exam, and some questions asked us to find and fix mistakes in work an AI had produced.” This is only an example. How you use it, and the outcome, are your own decision and responsibility.
What if I disagree with my result?
Appeal within 14 days of the result. There is no fee.
What personal data do you keep?
For the notification list, only your email address and the grade and language you chose. When you register for the exam, your name, date of birth and email address, and a phone number only if you choose to give one. Stored data is encrypted. We do not collect ID documents or face video. Email us to have your data deleted.
How do I contact you?
Email contact@iapqb.org. We reply within 3 working days. New reports and notices are also posted on X (@iapqb).
