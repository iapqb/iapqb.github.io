What this qualification checks

Using AI at work is ordinary now. What an employer still needs to know is whether the person can check what the AI produced and stand behind the result. Every grade of IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI gives its highest weight to one question: can you verify AI output? The qualification does not guarantee any job or hiring outcome.

How the exam works

  • Online, in Korea or abroad. You need a computer with a stable internet connection and a camera for the ID video call. The languages each grade is offered in are stated in the examination notice.
  • ID check by live video. Just before you start, we compare your photo ID with you on a live video call. Cover your national ID number when you show the card. We do not store ID images, face video or screen recordings, and we do not use automatic face matching. We record only that the check was done and when.
  • AI tools. In practical exams you may use generative AI without restriction and submit a short log of the tools and key prompts you used; the log is used only if misconduct is investigated and is not scored. For written exams, the tools you may use are stated in the examination notice. Help from another person, and photographing, sharing or posting questions, is misconduct.
  • Results. Auto-scored results appear on screen as soon as you finish. Official results follow within 5 business days.

Certificates and verification

If you pass, you receive a digital certificate that can be checked for forgery or alteration (Open Badges compatible). We do not make plastic cards, to reduce plastic waste. Anyone can check a certificate at any time on our verification page using its number. A certificate is valid for 3 years and can be renewed.

Exam deadline and refunds

  • Start your exam within 90 days of payment. Ask before the deadline and we extend it once by 90 days, free. A registration not started by the deadline lapses without refund; we remind you 7 days and 1 day before.
  • Cancel any time before you start and we refund the full fee within 3 business days. The exam counts as started when the first question appears on your screen; after that there is no refund.
  • How to request a refund: press “Cancel registration” on your registration screen, or email contact@iapqb.org with your name, payment date and grade. We refund by cancelling the card approval.
  • If we cancel, postpone or have a system failure, you choose a full refund or a new sitting. Documented emergencies such as hospitalization or a death in the family are refunded in full.
