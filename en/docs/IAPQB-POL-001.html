IAPQB-POL-001

Fees, Refunds and Privacy Policy

This is an English translation of the Korean original. If the translation differs from the Korean original, the Korean original prevails. “IAPQB” means 국제AI실무자격검정원 (International AI Practice Qualification Board).
The fee and refund provisions of this policy (Chapters 1 and 2) are aligned with Schedule 1 and Articles 26 and 27 of IAPQB-GOV-002. Where they differ, IAPQB-GOV-002 applies; however, where this policy is more favourable to the candidate, this policy applies. For Chapter 3 (Personal Information), see the Addenda.

How to request a refund

Press “Cancel registration” on your registration screen, or email contact@iapqb.org with your name, payment date and grade. We refund by cancelling the card approval within 3 business days (Article 6). The refund rules are in Article 4-3; grades and fees are on the registration page.

Chapter 1 Fees

Article 1 (Disclosure principle) IAPQB has no fees other than the amounts published on its official website. IAPQB does not, under any circumstances, demand or accept money under any name that has not been published.

Article 2 (Basis of fees) IAPQB’s fees follow Article 25 of IAPQB-GOV-002 and its Schedule 1, which is published together with the full text of IAPQB-GOV-002. IAPQB does not charge or accept any money until the time set by Articles 4 and 26 of that regulation, and any change of fees follows Article 25(3) of that regulation.

Article 3 (No educational services) IAPQB does not offer or sell courses, lectures or textbooks. Separating education from assessment is an operating principle of IAPQB (IAPQB-GOV-001 Article 6).

Chapter 2 Refunds

Article 4 (Registration)
(1) IAPQB does not issue vouchers, passes or any other token to be presented and used later.
(2) Registration is complete when the candidate pays the examination fee during the registration period of a session announced under Article 18(1) of IAPQB-GOV-002; the session, qualification and grade are fixed at that time.
(3) In a session run on demand (IAPQB-POL-002 Article 3(5)), the candidate must start the examination within 90 days from the day registration is completed (this period is called the “exam deadline”). If the candidate asks before the exam deadline ends, IAPQB extends it once by 90 days, without charge. IAPQB notifies the candidate 7 days and 1 day before the exam deadline ends.
(4) A registration for which the examination has not been started by the exam deadline lapses when the deadline passes, and IAPQB does not refund the fee. However, where Article 4-3(4) or (5) applies, that provision governs.
(5) A registration may not be transferred to another person.

Article 4-2 (When the examination starts)
(1) The examination is deemed to have started when the first question is displayed to the candidate. The time is determined by IAPQB’s server record and is also shown on the candidate’s screen. This constitutes a case where “the provision of services has commenced” under Article 17(2)(5) of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc. of the Republic of Korea.
(2) Under Article 17(6) of that Act, IAPQB displays on screen, and obtains the candidate’s confirmation of, Article 4(3) and (4) and Article 4-3(1) to (3) before payment, and paragraph (1) and Article 4-3(3) immediately before the examination starts. IAPQB also publishes sample questions for each grade so that anyone can see them before paying.

Article 4-3 (Refund of examination fees)
(1) In a session run on demand, the candidate may cancel the registration at any time before starting the examination, until the exam deadline passes, and IAPQB refunds the full fee. Because the registration period of an on-demand session runs until the exam deadline, a cancellation under this paragraph is a “cancellation within the registration period” under Article 27(1)(1) of IAPQB-GOV-002.
(2) In any other session, refunds on cancellation are as follows (IAPQB-GOV-002 Article 27(1)). However, if the candidate cancels within 7 days of paying the fee and before starting the examination, the full fee is refunded regardless of the items below (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Etc., Article 17(1)).

1. Cancelled within the registration period: full refund

2. Cancelled up to 7 days before the examination date: full refund

3. Cancelled up to 3 days before the examination date: 50% refund

4. After the time limit in item 3: no refund

(3) After the examination has started, no refund is made, including where the candidate ends the examination early or does not submit answers (Article 4-2). However, paragraphs (4) and (5) apply where relevant.
(4) Where an examination is cancelled or postponed for reasons attributable to IAPQB, or interrupted by a system failure, Article 27(2) of IAPQB-GOV-002 applies.
(5) Refunds and carry-overs where the candidate provides proof of an unavoidable reason, such as a natural disaster or the candidate’s own illness or injury, follow Article 27(3) of IAPQB-GOV-002 and Article 20 of IAPQB-POL-002.
(6) Time limits in this policy end at 24:00 Korea time (UTC+09:00) on the day concerned. Business days follow Article 3(4) of IAPQB-POL-002.

Article 6 (Processing time) Refunds are processed within 3 business days from the day the grounds for the refund are confirmed.

Chapter 3 Personal Information

Article 7 (Minimum collection)
(1) IAPQB collects only the minimum information needed for examinations, issuing certificates and verification: name, date of birth, contact number and email address. IAPQB does not collect resident registration numbers unless a law provides a basis for it.
(2) IAPQB does not collect or store biometric information such as faces or fingerprints, images of ID documents, or recordings made during the examination (IAPQB-POL-002 Article 6(3) to (5)). Identity is checked only by live comparison, and only the fact and time of the check are recorded.
(3) IAPQB does not process sensitive information (Personal Information Protection Act, Article 23). If it ever becomes necessary to process it, IAPQB will first amend this policy and publish the items and the legal basis, and only then collect it.
(4) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), for notification sign-ups under Article 10-6 IAPQB receives only the email address and the grade of interest and language chosen by the applicant, and does not receive name, date of birth or phone number.

Article 8 (Use and disclosure) Personal information collected is used only for conducting examinations, issuing and verifying qualifications, and meeting legal obligations; notification sign-up information is used only for the purpose in Article 10-6(2). It is not provided to third parties without the data subject’s consent or a legal basis.

Article 9 (Retention and destruction)
(1) Examination and issuance records are kept for 5 years (GOV-001 Article 10); other information is destroyed without delay once its purpose has been achieved. The minimum information needed to verify a qualification (name, qualification number, issue date and validity period) is kept for the validity period of the qualification.
(2) Destruction procedure: personal information whose retention period has ended, or whose purpose has been achieved, is selected and destroyed once a month. Electronic files are permanently deleted by a method that prevents recovery; printed material is shredded or incinerated.
(3) The 5-year retention in the first sentence of paragraph (1) is based on the Framework Act on Qualifications and its subordinate statutes and on IAPQB’s regulations (GOV-001 Article 10). It applies only to records of registration, scoring and pass/fail decisions, and the issuance and revocation of qualifications.

Article 9-2 (Outsourcing of processing)
(1) IAPQB may outsource the processing of personal information for payment processing, website hosting, receiving, sending and storing email, operating the examination system, and live video checks.
(2) When outsourcing begins, IAPQB states the name of the processor and the work outsourced in this policy and publishes it on the website. Processors currently engaged: Zoho Corporation, for receiving and storing email (contact@iapqb.org) and sending notification emails; Cloudflare, Inc., for encrypting, transforming and storing notification sign-up data (Article 10-6).
(3) IAPQB supervises and trains its processors as required by Article 26 of the Personal Information Protection Act and keeps records of the results.

Article 9-3 (Security measures)
(1) IAPQB takes the measures required by Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act to prevent personal information from leaking.
(2) Technical measures: encryption in transit (HTTPS at all times), encryption at rest, least-privilege access, and retention of access logs protected against forgery and alteration.
(3) Administrative measures: an internal management plan, reviewed at least once a year, and designated persons who handle personal information, kept to the minimum.
(4) Designing collection to the minimum (Article 7) is the first of the measures in this Article. Information that is not collected cannot leak.

Article 9-4 (Overseas transfer)
(1) Where personal information is processed or stored overseas because of outsourcing, IAPQB states in this policy, and publishes on the website, the items transferred, the destination country, when and how they are transferred, the name and contact details of the recipient, the recipient’s purpose of use and retention period, the legal basis, and how to refuse the transfer and the consequences of refusing (Personal Information Protection Act, Article 28-8).
(2) Current overseas transfers:
(a) Items: name, email address and message content sent to IAPQB’s email, and the recipient addresses of notification emails sent by IAPQB. Country: United States. When and how: over the network when email is sent or received. Recipient: Zoho Corporation (privacy@zohocorp.com). Purpose: receiving, sending and storing email. Retention: the period in Article 9(1) (for notification emails, the period in Article 10-6(3)).
(b) Items: for notification sign-ups, the email address (encrypted with IAPQB’s public key on arrival; what is stored is the ciphertext and a value transformed with a secret key), grade of interest, language, sign-up time, processing order, and a secret-key transformed value of the connecting IP address. Country: United States and the countries where Cloudflare data centres are located (preferred storage location: Asia-Pacific, which Cloudflare does not guarantee). When and how: over the network at sign-up. Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc. (sar@cloudflare.com). Purpose: processing sign-ups, encrypted storage, duplicate checks and abuse prevention. Retention: the period in Article 10-6(3) (for the IP value, paragraph (4) of that Article).
(3) The overseas transfers in paragraph (2) are outsourcing and storage under Article 28-8(1)(3) of the Personal Information Protection Act. A data subject who does not want their information transferred overseas may choose not to use IAPQB’s email or not to sign up for notifications; in that case they cannot receive those services.

Article 10 (Access, correction and deletion) A data subject may at any time ask for access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of processing of their information, and IAPQB acts within 10 days.

Article 10-2 (Aggregating responses to the anonymous self-check tool)
(1) The self-check tool published on IAPQB’s website is used without sign-up or log-in, and IAPQB does not link responses to any individual.
(2) IAPQB stores only the option chosen for each question, the date of the response, and the domain of the website the visitor came from. It does not store names, contact details, IP addresses, cookie identifiers or device fingerprints.
(3) The responses in paragraph (2) are used only as a basis for designing examination criteria and for producing aggregate statistics.
(4) Because respondents give no information that identifies them, IAPQB cannot later find and delete an individual response. IAPQB says so on the tool’s screen in advance.

Article 10-3 (Publishing and providing aggregate statistics)
(1) IAPQB may publish statistics aggregated from the responses in Article 10-2, or provide them to third parties.
(2) Statistics under paragraph (1) are produced only in a form that cannot identify individuals. Individual response records are never provided to third parties.
(3) IAPQB rounds published aggregate statistics to the nearest 100. Items with fewer than 100 responses are not published, because respondents in small samples could be re-identified.
(4) IAPQB states the reference date and the sample size with any statistics it publishes.

Article 10-4 (Web analytics tool)
(1) IAPQB may use an external analytics tool for website traffic statistics.
(2) The tool in paragraph (1) is limited to one that does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors.
(3) IAPQB states the name of the tool in use on the website.

Article 10-5 (Notifying and reporting breaches)
(1) When IAPQB becomes aware that personal information has been leaked, lost, stolen, forged, altered or damaged (a “breach”), it notifies the data subjects concerned without delay of the items involved; when and how the breach happened; what the data subject can do to reduce harm; IAPQB’s response and remedy procedure; the person and contact details for reports; and the data subject’s legal rights, such as compensation and dispute mediation, and how to exercise them.
(2) Even before a breach is confirmed, when IAPQB becomes aware that one may have occurred, it notifies without delay every data subject who may be affected of that fact and of information to reduce harm. It does not wait until its investigation is finished (Personal Information Protection Act Article 34(2), in force from 11 September 2026).
(3) IAPQB prepares and takes measures to minimise harm, including measures to stop the harm spreading, such as retrieving or deleting the personal information involved.
(4) IAPQB reports without delay to the Personal Information Protection Commission or a specialised agency as the law requires.
(5) IAPQB records the handling under paragraphs (1) to (4) and publishes a summary on the website in a form that does not identify individuals.

Article 10-6 (Notification sign-up)
(1) When a data subject signs up on the website, IAPQB notifies them by email that registration has opened for the session they signed up for. The notification contains a link to priority registration, which opens one day before public registration starts, and is sent before priority registration opens. Priority registration opens at the same time for everyone who signed up; its period and method are stated with the registration period in the examination notice for that session. The order of sign-up does not affect eligibility, scoring or passing.
(2) The items collected are the email address and the grade of interest and language chosen by the applicant; the sign-up time and processing order are also recorded. The only purpose is the notification in paragraph (1). IAPQB obtains the data subject’s consent before collecting; the data subject may refuse, in which case they cannot receive the notification. Children under 14 cannot sign up.
(3) The data is kept until 30 days after registration for the session concerned closes, or until 31 December 2027 if registration for that session has not opened by then. When the period ends, the data is destroyed without delay; if the data subject cancels, it is destroyed immediately. Destroyed values remain in the recovery records of Cloudflare’s database for up to 30 days before being erased automatically, and cannot be read without IAPQB’s private key.
(4) IAPQB encrypts the email address with a public key it controls as soon as it is received and stores only the ciphertext; the private key needed to decrypt it is kept only on IAPQB’s office computer. Duplicate checks use only a value transformed with a secret key. To prevent abuse, IAPQB processes a secret-key transformed value of the connecting IP address (Personal Information Protection Act Article 15(1)(6)); that value is deleted when the next sign-up is processed after 24 hours have passed, and at the latest together with the destruction under paragraph (3). Country information at sign-up is kept only as totals per country, not linked to any individual.
(5) IAPQB does not send advertising using notification sign-up information.
(6) The data subject may cancel the sign-up on the website at any time; IAPQB does not require log-in or identity verification for this.
(7) If IAPQB publishes the number of sign-ups, it publishes only the total, and only when there are 30 or more.

Chapter 4 Enquiries and Disputes

Article 11 (Contact)
(1) Enquiries about fees, refunds and personal information are received through the public channel posted on IAPQB’s website.
(2) IAPQB’s email channel is contact@iapqb.org, posted on the website.
(3) The Chief Privacy Officer is Lee Seung-won, IAPQB’s representative, reachable through the channel in paragraph (2). Because IAPQB falls under the proviso to Article 31(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act and does not designate a separate officer, the representative is the Chief Privacy Officer under paragraph (2) of that Article.
(4) If an amendment to the Presidential Decree changes the criteria behind paragraph (3) so that IAPQB must designate an officer, IAPQB will amend this Article when it designates one and publish the officer’s name and contact details.

Article 12 (Dispute resolution) Consumer disputes are handled with reference to the Consumer Dispute Resolution Criteria (Fair Trade Commission notice). If no agreement is reached, IAPQB guides the consumer to the mediation procedures of the Korea Consumer Agency or other relevant bodies.
