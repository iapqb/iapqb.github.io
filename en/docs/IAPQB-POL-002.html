IAPQB-POL-002

Examination Terms

This is an English translation of the Korean original. If the translation differs from the Korean original, the Korean original prevails. “IAPQB” means 국제AI실무자격검정원 (International AI Practice Qualification Board).
These terms take effect on the date of the first examination notice (Addenda). Where they differ from IAPQB-GOV-002, IAPQB-GOV-002 applies; however, where these terms are more favourable to the candidate, these terms apply.

Chapter 1 General Provisions

Article 1 (Purpose) These terms set out matters concerning registration, sitting, scoring, announcement of results and appeals for examinations conducted by IAPQB.

Article 2 (Scope)
(1) These terms apply to examinations conducted by IAPQB.
(2) IAPQB does not offer or sell courses, lectures or textbooks (IAPQB-POL-001 Article 3).
(3) These terms do not apply to the free self-check (Readiness Check). The self-check is not an examination, and no certificate is issued for it.
(4) Fees, refunds and personal information are governed by IAPQB-POL-001. However, Article 18(6) and Article 20 of these terms take precedence over Article 4-3 of that document.

Article 3 (Definitions)
(1) “Session” means a unit in which registration, sitting and announcement of results are carried out under one examination notice.
(2) “Auto-scored subject” means a subject made up only of multiple-choice or short-answer questions, or of structured response tasks to which a scoring rubric can be applied mechanically.
(3) “Work product” means the result a candidate submits for a practical task, together with its verification report.
(4) “Business day” means a day other than Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays under the Regulations on Holidays of Government Offices. Dates and time limits in these terms are based on Korea time (UTC+09:00).
(5) “On-demand testing” means a method in which registration and sitting are accepted every day during a period set by one examination notice. In an on-demand session, “session” in these terms means each candidate’s registration; “session implementation plan” means that examination notice; “registration closing date” means the day before the candidate starts the examination; “last examination date of the session” and “examination date” mean the day the candidate sat the examination; and “transferring the registration to the next session” means letting the candidate sit again under the same registration, with a new exam deadline (IAPQB-POL-001 Article 4(3)) counted from that day. However, “session” in Article 11(4) and (5) means a calendar month.

Chapter 2 Registration and Sitting

Article 4 (Eligibility)
(1) Foundation and Associate have no eligibility requirements.
(2) Professional is open to Associate holders, and to persons who provide proof of work experience meeting the criteria announced in the session implementation plan under Article 13(4) of IAPQB-GOV-002.
(3) Strategist is open to Professional holders.
(4) A person barred from sitting under Article 18 may not sit during that period.

Article 5 (Registration)
(1) Registration is made only through IAPQB’s website (including mirror addresses and the payment and examination systems posted on the website).
(2) At registration, the candidate provides their name, date of birth, contact number and email address.
(3) Errors in registration details may be corrected until the registration closing date. Corrections after that date are made only if supporting documents are submitted.
(4) The examination fee is paid with a payment method in the candidate’s own name (IAPQB-GOV-002 Article 19(1)).

Article 6 (Identity check)
(1) Before the examination starts, the candidate’s identity is checked against a photo ID.
(2) A candidate who refuses the identity check or does not present ID may not sit the examination.
(3) The identity check is done only by live comparison. IAPQB does not store ID images, face video or screen recordings. It stores only the fact that the check was completed and the time.
(4) When presenting ID, the candidate covers unique identification numbers such as the resident registration number. IAPQB does not ask for unique identification numbers and does not record them even if they appear on screen.
(5) IAPQB does not use technical means that match faces automatically, so that no biometric information (Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act, Article 18(3)) is created during the identity check.
(6) The identity check is done by video call, viewing the ID and the candidate’s face together; under paragraph (3) the video is not stored. The tool, the time and the equipment needed for the check are announced in the session implementation plan.

Article 7 (Examination environment)
(1) Examinations are conducted online, and the candidate sits in an environment with a stable internet connection. IAPQB does not film or record the screen or the candidate during the examination.
(2) In place of filming, IAPQB determines impersonation and misconduct through the live identity comparison under Article 19 of IAPQB-GOV-002 and the examination system records; applies random assignment of industry scenarios under Article 13 of IAPQB-STD-002 to the Strategist written-response examination; and, at every grade, scores the traces of verification left in the work product. IAPQB judged that recordings would add to candidates’ personal information burden without a matching gain in the ability to detect misconduct.
(3) If the examination is interrupted by a failure of the candidate’s connection, reconnection is allowed for the remaining time only. If the total interruption exceeds 20 percent of the examination time, the sitting is void and the registration is transferred to the next session.
(4) If the examination is interrupted by a failure of IAPQB’s system, IAPQB may continue it on a backup system specified in the session implementation plan. If it cannot continue, the session is void, and IAPQB refunds the full fee or, at the candidate’s choice, transfers the registration to the next session.

Article 8 (Accommodations)
(1) A candidate who needs accommodations because of a disability or illness may apply by the registration closing date.
(2) IAPQB provides accommodations such as extra time, screen magnification and the use of assistive devices.
(3) IAPQB replies with its decision within 7 days of the application.

Chapter 3 Scoring

Article 9 (Types of scoring)
(1) Written and practical examinations are scored automatically.
(2) Written-response examinations are scored by markers.
(3) Scoring under paragraphs (1) and (2) applies the scoring rubric published before the examination. Results under paragraph (2) are reviewed by one member of the examination staff under Article 9 of IAPQB-GOV-002 who was not involved in scoring that session (IAPQB-GOV-002 Article 15(5)).
(4) IAPQB does not replace markers’ judgement with artificial intelligence.

Article 10 (Pass mark)
(1) The pass mark is as set by Article 16 of IAPQB-GOV-002 and is posted with the examination notice.
(2) IAPQB does not change the pass mark from session to session. If a change is needed, it is announced 30 days before the first examination date after the change.

Article 11 (Time limits for results)
(1) Scores for auto-scored subjects are shown on screen as soon as the examination ends. Official results are announced within 5 business days from the last examination date of the session.
(2) Results for Strategist, assessed by written-response examination, are announced within 45 days from the last examination date of the session.
(3) If IAPQB cannot meet the time limit in paragraph (1) or (2), it announces the delay and the new announcement date on the website before the time limit expires.
(4) If the time limit in paragraph (2) is exceeded in two consecutive sessions of the same grade, IAPQB refunds the full fee to every candidate of that session.
(5) IAPQB publishes, with the results of every session, the actual number of days it took to announce them.

Article 12 (Notification of results)
(1) Results are notified by posting them on the candidate screen of IAPQB’s website.
(2) IAPQB does not answer phone or email enquiries about an individual candidate’s scoring details. Objections are handled under Article 19.
(3) When a third party asks to verify a qualification, IAPQB only explains how to check it by qualification number on the verification page, and does not give out the candidate’s name, scores or other personal information (IAPQB-GOV-002 Articles 21(2) and 30).

Chapter 4 Use of Generative AI

Article 13 (Written examinations) The extent to which artificial intelligence tools such as generative AI, and other external tools (including dictionaries and translators), may be used in written examinations is set by the session implementation plan (for AI tools, IAPQB-GOV-002 Article 17). Use beyond that extent is misconduct under Article 17 of these terms (IAPQB-GOV-002 Article 17 proviso and Article 20(1)(4)).

Article 14 (Practical examinations)
(1) The use of generative AI is not restricted in practical examinations.
(2) The candidate submits a usage log stating the name and version of each tool used, the key prompts, and the parts of the work product generated by AI.
(3) IAPQB does not deduct points for using AI. What is assessed is whether the work product can be verified, whatever tools were used.
(4) The usage log in paragraph (2) is used only as material for investigating misconduct under Article 17 and is not reflected in scoring. Scoring is limited to the traces of verification left in the submitted work product itself.
(5) Paragraph (4) applies to practical examinations the principle that IAPQB does not decide qualifications on the basis of self-reports (IAPQB-GOV-001). Because IAPQB cannot confirm the accuracy of what candidates report about themselves, such reports cannot be a basis for scores.

Chapter 5 Submissions

Article 15 (Scope of submissions)
(1) Work products must not contain third parties’ trade secrets or personal information.
(2) A candidate who submits work prepared for their organisation removes identifying information and also submits a statement that the organisation consented.
(3) Submissions that breach paragraph (1) are not scored, and the sitting for that session is void.

Article 16 (Rights in submissions)
(1) Copyright in a submission belongs to the candidate.
(2) IAPQB uses submissions only for scoring, review, handling appeals and investigating misconduct.
(3) If IAPQB wishes to publish all or part of a submission, it obtains the candidate’s written consent. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

Chapter 6 Misconduct

Article 17 (Misconduct) The following are misconduct:

1. Sitting on behalf of another person, or making false statements about eligibility

2. Photographing, recording, transmitting or posting examination questions or answers

3. Submitting work prepared by another person

4. Communicating with another person, sharing the screen, or allowing remote control during the examination

5. Failing to submit the usage log under Article 14(2), or submitting it untruthfully

6. Forging or altering an ID

Article 18 (Measures)
(1) Where misconduct is confirmed, the sitting for that session is void.
(2) Any qualification already obtained is revoked.
(3) The person may not sit IAPQB examinations for 2 years from the date of voiding or revocation (IAPQB-GOV-002 Article 20(2)).
(4) When a qualification is revoked, its number and the revocation date are shown on the verification page. The holder’s name is not shown.
(5) A person subject to a measure under paragraphs (1) to (3) may appeal within 14 days of being notified of it.
(6) Fees for a session voided or revoked because of misconduct are not refunded.

Chapter 7 Appeals

Article 19 (Appeals)
(1) An appeal may be made within 14 days of the announcement of results.
(2) For auto-scored subjects, only errors in processing the scoring may be appealed. IAPQB replies with the result of checking the answers within 7 days of the appeal.
(3) For written-response questions and work products, only errors in applying the published rubric may be appealed. The marker’s judgement itself cannot be appealed.
(4) Re-marking under paragraph (3) is done by a marker who did not take part in the original marking, and IAPQB replies within 14 days of the appeal.
(5) No fee is charged for appeals or re-marking (IAPQB-GOV-002 Article 25(1)).
(6) If re-marking lowers the score, the original score applies.

Chapter 8 Special Cases for Cancellation

Article 20 (Special cases)
(1) Notwithstanding Article 4-3 of IAPQB-POL-001, the full fee is refunded, even after the examination date, if any of the following occurs:

1. The candidate is hospitalised or quarantined

2. The candidate’s spouse, lineal ascendant or descendant, or sibling dies

3. The candidate is isolated under the Infectious Disease Control and Prevention Act

4. Sitting was impossible because of a natural disaster, an internet shutdown in the candidate’s area, or a comparable reason

(2) A refund under paragraph (1) is requested by submitting supporting documents within 30 days of the examination date. An internet shutdown may be evidenced by published measurement records and screenshots sent by the candidate.
(3) IAPQB replies with the result within 7 days of the request.

Chapter 9 Supplementary Provisions

Article 21 (Changes to the terms)
(1) IAPQB may change these terms.
(2) Changed terms are announced on the website 30 days before they take effect, together with the articles before and after the change.
(3) For a session for which registration has already been completed, the terms in force at the time of registration apply.

Article 22 (Governing law and jurisdiction) Disputes concerning these terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea, and jurisdiction follows the Civil Procedure Act.

Article 23 (Enquiries) Enquiries about these terms are received through the contact channel on IAPQB’s website.

Addenda

These terms take effect on the date of IAPQB’s first examination notice.
