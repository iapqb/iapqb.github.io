Partner terms

For training providers, coaching institutes, schools, universities and employers with a group registration agreement with IAPQB.

Published 22 November 2026 · Korean original: 파트너 약관

1. Scope

  1. These terms apply to organisations that have a group registration agreement with IAPQB ("partners") and form part of each agreement.
  2. A partner that sends a list of learners or pays fees under an agreement accepts these terms.
  3. Where an agreement says something different, the agreement applies. An agreement cannot depart from the Regulations on Managing and Operating the Qualification (IAPQB-GOV-002, Korean), the Examination Terms (IAPQB-POL-002) or the Fees, Refunds and Privacy Policy (IAPQB-POL-001).

2. What IAPQB does

  1. IAPQB runs and marks the exam and issues the credential.
  2. IAPQB does not sell or approve courses or materials, and does not say that a particular course is needed to sit the exam or makes it easier to pass.

3. Group registration

  1. The partner sends a list of learners' email addresses and pays fees under the agreement.
  2. IAPQB gives the partner an agreement code. The code only confirms the agreement. It is not an exam entitlement and cannot be sold, passed on or published.
  3. Each listed learner registers separately with their listed email address. Deadlines and extensions are the same as for individual candidates (IAPQB-POL-001).
  4. Group reductions are set by agreement within the limits of the Regulations (IAPQB-GOV-002). Fees under an agreement never exceed the published fee for each grade.
  5. If the partner charges learners for the exam, it shows the exam fee separately from course fees and does not charge more than the published fee.

4. Advertising

  1. Describe the credential as a private qualification registered under the Framework Act on Qualifications of the Republic of Korea (registration No. 2026-008253). Do not present it as a national or government-accredited qualification.
  2. Do not use "official IAPQB course", "IAPQB-certified course", "IAPQB-approved course", guaranteed pass, guaranteed job or salary, pass rates, rankings or numbers of successful candidates.
  3. Do not say that a course is needed to sit the exam or makes it easier to pass.
  4. Use a successful learner's name or photo only with that learner's written consent.
  5. Anyone paid, given free exams or given discounts to promote your course must label the post as an advertisement at the start, where it is easy to see. In India this follows the ASCI influencer guidelines and the CCPA guidelines on coaching advertisements.
  6. Do not describe the relationship with IAPQB as a memorandum of understanding (MoU), accreditation, franchise or official partnership. You may say that your learners can register for the IAPQB exam as a group.
  7. Do not use the IAPQB logo. You may write the name in plain text.

5. Referrals

If IAPQB offers referral rewards, they are single-level: no reward is paid for recruiting other partners or referrers. Partners do not appoint sub-resellers or sub-referrers.

6. Personal data

  1. Partners send IAPQB only what registration needs (learners' email addresses) and obtain each learner's consent before sending it.
  2. IAPQB processes personal data under IAPQB-POL-001.
  3. IAPQB gives a partner a learner's results, by group, only with that learner's consent.

7. Refunds

Refunds of fees paid under an agreement follow Article 4-3 of IAPQB-POL-001 for each registration. Fees the partner paid are returned to the partner's payment method.

8. Breach

  1. If a partner breaches these terms, IAPQB may ask it to correct the breach.
  2. If the partner does not correct it, or the breach is serious (such as selling agreement codes or advertising an "official course" or a guaranteed pass), IAPQB may end the agreement and stop accepting new registrations under it. Learners already registered keep their registration and results.

9. Changes, governing law and language

  1. IAPQB announces changes on this page 30 days before they take effect.
  2. These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea, and jurisdiction follows the Civil Procedure Act.
