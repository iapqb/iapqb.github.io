IAPQB Notice No. 2026-1 · Date of notice: 19 November 2026

Under Article 18(1) of the Rules on the Management and Operation of the Qualification (IAPQB-GOV-002), IAPQB gives notice that examinations for the Foundation, Associate and Professional grades will run on demand, as follows.

2. Grades and exam methods

GradeMethodSubjectsQuestions · time

AssociatePractical (task-based)Work automation · Output verification2 tasks, 90 min

Written and practical exams are scored automatically against rubrics published in advance. Practical answers are submitted in formats to which the rubric can be applied mechanically, such as selection, ordering, error identification and numeric entry. Each candidate's paper is newly assembled from a non-public item bank, with the set number of items for each subject.

Strategist will be announced separately as scheduled sessions. Because only Professional holders are eligible (GOV-002 Article 13(3)), it will be announced after the first candidates obtain Professional.

(GOV-002 Articles 14 and 15 · STD-002 Articles 5 and 9)

3. Exam period and how to sit

  • Exam period: on demand from Saturday 19 December 2026
  • Place: online (IAPQB examination system). All you need is a computer with an internet connection.
  • Exam language: Foundation, Associate and Professional are all offered in Korean and English. You choose the language when you register; the paper structure and the scoring rubric are the same in both languages. The certificate is issued in the language you sat. Candidates who use other languages may sit with a translator, including the browser's automatic translation (see 8).
  • Steps: register (confirm your email address) → payment → start the exam whenever you like before your deadline → auto-scored result shown at once → official result posted within 5 business days
  • IAPQB does not photograph or record your screen or you during the exam.
  • If the exam is cut off by a connection problem on your side, you can reconnect within the remaining time. If the interruptions add up to more than 20% of the exam time, that sitting is void and your registration is moved to a new sitting at no extra cost.

(GOV-002 Articles 18(2) and 19 · POL-002 Articles 6, 7 and 11(1))

4. Registration

  • Registration opens: Friday 18 December 2026 at 10:00 (people who signed up for the first-session notice) and Saturday 19 December 2026 at 10:00 (everyone), Korea time [in India: 06:30 IST]. After that, registration stays open.
  • How: only on the IAPQB website (including its payment and exam system).
  • Information you give: name · date of birth · contact number · email address
  • Payment is processed by Dodo Payments, an overseas payment provider that sells the exam on IAPQB's behalf. Your card statement and receipt may show the name Dodo Payments. Candidates in Korea pay in Korean won; candidates outside Korea pay in the currency shown on the payment screen, and that country's taxes may be added.
  • Exam deadline: 90 days from payment. If you ask before the deadline, it is extended once by 90 days at no cost. A registration not started by the deadline lapses without a refund (reminders 7 days and 1 day before).
  • A registration cannot be transferred to another person.
  • Sample questions and the exam specifications for each grade will be published on the website before registration opens.

(GOV-002 Article 19(1) · POL-002 Article 5 · POL-001 Articles 4 and 4-2(2))

5. Eligibility

  • Foundation · Associate: none
  • Professional: Associate holders, or people who prove equivalent work experience
    • Standard: at least 2 years of experience in the relevant role, including applying generative AI tools at work
    • Documents: one proof of the length of your experience (a copy with national ID numbers hidden) and a work statement you write yourself (2 cases of applying AI, each up to 500 characters, with details identifying your employer removed)
    • Submit them before payment; IAPQB tells you the result within 7 days. Pay after you receive notice that you are eligible.
    • Submitted documents are destroyed without delay after checking; only the result and the date of checking are recorded.
  • People barred from sitting because of misconduct cannot sit during that period.

(GOV-002 Articles 13 and 29(1) · POL-002 Article 4(4))

6. Fees and refunds

GradeFee (Korean VAT included)

The certificate fee is included, and there are no charges beyond the published amounts. For candidates outside Korea, the fee is the KRW amount above converted at the exchange rate at the time of payment. If reductions by country or region are set, they will be announced separately.

Refunds

  • Before you start, you can cancel at any time within your exam deadline and receive the full fee back.
  • The exam counts as started when the first question appears on your screen (the time follows the server record and is also shown on screen). After that there is no refund, even if you stop midway or do not submit answers. You confirm this rule on screen before payment and again just before you start.
  • If IAPQB cancels or postpones an exam, or it stops because of a system failure, you choose a full refund or a new sitting.
  • If you prove an unavoidable reason, such as a natural disaster or your own illness or injury, IAPQB may refund all or part of the fee or move your exam deadline.
  • Refunds are processed within 3 business days from the day the reason is confirmed.

(GOV-002 Articles 25, 27 and 28 and [Annex 1] · POL-001 Articles 4-2, 4-3 and 6)

Pass marks

  • Foundation: 60 or more out of 100 in the written exam
  • Associate: 60 or more out of 100 in each of the written and practical exams
  • Professional: 70 or more out of 100 in the practical exam
  • Scoring less than 40% of the points of any one subject is a fail
  • These pass marks are provisional and are not adjusted while exams are running. Any change is announced at least 30 days before the first exam date after the change.

(GOV-002 Articles 14(3) and 16 · STD-002 Articles 5, 6, 7, 12 and 20)

8. Use of AI tools

  • You may use tools, including generative AI, in both the written and the practical exams. Dictionaries and translators (including the browser's automatic translation) are also allowed. This qualification checks your ability to use AI in real work, so using a tool is never in itself a reason to deduct points.
  • Entering the questions into an AI tool is permitted use. Sending or posting questions or answers to a person, or photographing or recording them, is misconduct.
  • In the practical exam you submit a usage record: the names and versions of the tools you used, your main prompts, and which parts of your output AI produced. The usage record does not count toward your score and is used for misconduct investigations; for registrations from 21 December 2026 it is also used, under v1.8 of the Examination Terms, to improve questions and for statistics and research that identify no one (added 21 November 2026). Scoring is based on the traces of verification left in your output.
  • This is an online exam without a proctor watching your screen. Impersonation and misconduct are judged by the exam system's records and your work.

(GOV-002 Articles 17, 19(4) and 20 · POL-002 Articles 13, 14 and 16)

9. How we identify candidates

  • IAPQB identifies you by the email address you register with. There is no identity document check and no video call, and you do not need a camera.
  • Your certificate shows your name exactly as you entered it at registration.
  • IAPQB does not receive images of identity documents, face video or screen recordings, and does not ask for national identification numbers such as the Korean resident registration number.
  • Having someone else sit the exam for you is misconduct (see 10).

(GOV-002 Article 19 · POL-002 Article 6)

10. Misconduct

The following are misconduct: impersonation or false statements about eligibility; photographing or recording exam questions or answers, or sending or posting them to anyone; submitting work made by someone else; contacting others, sharing your screen or allowing remote control during the exam; and not submitting the practical usage record or submitting a false one. If misconduct is confirmed, the sitting is void, you cannot sit for 2 years from the date it is voided or cancelled, and any qualification already issued is cancelled. Before a decision, IAPQB tells you the reasons and gives you a chance to explain, and you can appeal within 14 days from the day you are notified of the decision. Fees for sittings voided for misconduct are not refunded.

(GOV-002 Article 20 · POL-002 Articles 17 and 18)

11. Results and appeals

  • The auto-scored result appears on screen as soon as the exam ends, and the official result is posted on your candidate page within 5 business days from the exam date.
  • Appeals are made by email (contact@iapqb.org) within 14 days from the day you receive your result. For automatically scored subjects, an appeal covers only errors in processing the score, and IAPQB sends you the result of checking your answers within 7 days from your appeal. Appeals are free.

(GOV-002 Article 34 · POL-002 Articles 11(1) and 19)

12. Accommodations

If you need accommodations because of a disability or illness, you can apply before the exam. IAPQB provides extra time, screen magnification, use of assistive devices and similar support, and tells you its decision within 7 days from your application.

(POL-002 Article 8)

13. Certificates and validity

Successful candidates receive a digital certificate that can be checked for forgery or alteration (Open Badges compatible). IAPQB does not make physical cards, to reduce plastic; the qualification can be checked at any time on the credential check page of the IAPQB website. A certificate is valid for 3 years from issue and can be renewed by completing continuing education or passing a renewal assessment.

(GOV-002 Articles 21 and 23 · Decision log D-396)

14. Personal data

IAPQB collects only the name, date of birth, contact number and email address needed for the exam and for issuing and verifying the qualification, and does not collect Korean resident registration numbers. Exam and issue records are kept for 5 years. Details are in the privacy policy (IAPQB-POL-001, Chapter 3).

(GOV-002 Articles 29 and 31)
