Research reports

How courts, regulators, companies and researchers deal with AI at work.

IAPQB · Reports in English · Updated 24 November 2026

Each report sets out a problem, the documented cases behind it and what practitioners now do about it. The cases and the practices come from court decisions, regulators' documents, standards, company statements and peer-reviewed studies. Every source is opened and checked before publication, and anything that cannot be checked is left out.
