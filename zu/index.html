IAPQBCertifiedAppliedAI · iziqu ezizimele ezibhalisiwe, inombolo 2026-008253 (iRiphabhulikhi yaseKorea)

Ukubhalisa kuzovulwa ngemuva kwesaziso esisemthethweni sokuhlolwa. Okwamanje asamukeli zinkokhelo.

Ukuze uthole isixhumanisi sokubhalisa kuqala, usuku olulodwa ngaphambi kokuba ukubhalisa komphakathi kuvulwe, bhalisa lapha (ifomu lisesiNgisini).

Lezi ziqu zihlola ini

Ukusebenzisa i-AI emsebenzini sekuyinto ejwayelekile. Kodwa abaqashi basafuna ukwazi ukuthi umuntu ukuhlolile yini lokho i-AI ekukhiqizile, nokuthi angakwazi yini ukuma ngomphumela. Kuwo wonke amazinga, lokhu kuhlola yikona okunesisindo esikhulu kunakho konke. Lezi ziqu aziqinisekisi umsebenzi noma ukuqashwa.

Izimali zifaka i-VAT yaseKorea futhi zikhokhwa ngamawoni aseKorea (KRW). Amanani ngamadola ayalinganiselwa; umkhiphi wekhadi lakho uyawaguqula ngezinga lakhe. Isitifiketi sifakiwe futhi ayikho enye imali.

Ukuhlolwa kwenziwa kanjani

  • Ku-inthanethi, usuKorea noma uphesheya. Udinga ikhompyutha, i-inthanethi ezinzile nekhamera.
  • Ngaphambi nje kokuqala, sihlola umazisi wakho onesithombe ngekholi yevidiyo ebukhoma. Mboza inombolo yakho kamazisi kazwelonke. Asigcini izithombe zomazisi, ividiyo yobuso noma okurekhodwe esikrinini.
  • Ekuhlolweni okusebenzayo ungasebenzisa i-AI ekhiqizayo, bese uhambisa irekhodi elifushane lamathuluzi nama-prompt owasebenzisile. Amathuluzi avunyelwe ekuhlolweni okubhaliwe abalulwe esazisweni sokuhlolwa. Ukusizwa omunye umuntu noma ukwabelana ngemibuzo kuwukwephula imithetho.
  • Imiphumela emakwa ngokuzenzakalelayo ivela esikrinini uma uqeda, futhi imiphumela esemthethweni ilandela phakathi kwezinsuku zomsebenzi ezi-5. Izilimi zokuhlolwa kwezinga ngalinye zibalulwe esazisweni sokuhlolwa.

Inkokhelo

Khokha ngekhadi elisegameni lakho. Samukela amakhadi aseKorea nawamazwe ngamazwe (VISA, Mastercard, JCB, AMEX nokunye). Izinkokhelo zicutshungulwa yinkampani Eximbay Co., Ltd., ngakho isitatimende sekhadi lakho singabonisa igama elithi EXIMBAY.

Usuku lokugcina lokuhlolwa nokubuyiselwa imali

  • Qala ukuhlolwa kwakho phakathi kwezinsuku ezingu-90 kusukela ukhokhile. Uma ucela ngaphambi kosuku lokugcina, siyalunweba kanye ngezinsuku ezingu-90 mahhala. Ukubhalisa okungaqalanga ngosuku lokugcina kuyaphela ngaphandle kokubuyiselwa imali. Sizokukhumbuza izinsuku ezi-7 nosuku olu-1 ngaphambili.
  • Ungakhansela noma nini ngaphambi kokuqala bese ubuyiselwa yonke imali. Ukuhlolwa kuthathwa njengokuqalile lapho umbuzo wokuqala uvela esikrinini sakho; ngemuva kwalokho akukho kubuyiselwa imali.
  • Indlela yokucela ukubuyiselwa imali: cindezela inkinobho ethi “Cancel registration” esikrinini sakho sokubhalisa, noma uthumele i-imeyili ku-contact@iapqb.org ubhale igama lakho, usuku lokukhokha nezinga. Sikhansela inkokhelo yekhadi phakathi kwezinsuku zomsebenzi ezi-3.
  • Uma sikhansela noma sihlehlisa ukuhlolwa, noma uhlelo lwethu luwa, ukhetha ukubuyiselwa yonke imali noma ukuhlolwa kabusha. Uma ungakwazanga ukuhlolwa ngenxa yokulaliswa esibhedlela, ukuvalelwa (quarantine), ukushona kwelungu lomndeni eliseduze, inhlekelele yemvelo noma ukuvalwa kwe-inthanethi endaweni yakho, thumela ubufakazi phakathi kwezinsuku ezingu-30 ukuze ubuyiselwe yonke imali.
  • Strategist (izikhathi ezihleliwe): ubuyiselwa yonke imali kuze kube yizinsuku ezi-7 ngaphambi kosuku lokuhlolwa, u-50% kuze kube yizinsuku ezi-3 ngaphambili, ngemuva kwalokho akukho. Ukukhansela phakathi kwezinsuku ezi-7 ngemuva kokukhokha nangaphambi kokuhlolwa kubuyiselwa yonke imali njalo.

Izitifiketi

Uma uphasa, uthola isitifiketi sedijithali noma ubani angasiqinisekisa ngenombolo yaso kuwebhusayithi yethu (sihambisana ne-Open Badges). Ukuze sinciphise imfucuza yepulasitiki, asenzi amakhadi epulasitiki. Isitifiketi sisebenza iminyaka emi-3.
